# 📊 Product Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI Sales Dashboard** that provides comprehensive insights into product sales performance, revenue trends, customer behavior, and profitability. This dashboard helps businesses monitor KPIs, identify top-performing products, and make data-driven sales decisions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Product Sales Analysis Dashboard** transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights using Power BI. It enables stakeholders to analyze sales performance across products, categories, regions, customers, and time periods through interactive visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Analyze revenue and profit trends
- Identify top-performing products
- Track customer purchasing behavior
- Compare regional and category-wise sales
- Support strategic business decisions

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- 💰 Total Sales
- 📈 Total Profit
- 📦 Total Orders
- 🛒 Product Category Analysis
- 🏆 Top Selling Products
- 🌍 Region-wise Sales Performance
- 👥 Customer Analysis
- 📅 Monthly & Yearly Sales Trends
- 📊 Profit Margin Analysis
- 🎛️ Interactive Filters & Slicers

---
