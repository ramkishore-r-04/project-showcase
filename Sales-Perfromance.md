# 📈 Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI Sales Performance Dashboard** that provides comprehensive insights into sales trends, revenue, profitability, and business performance. This dashboard enables organizations to monitor key sales metrics, evaluate performance across regions and products, and make informed business decisions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Performance Dashboard** transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations. It helps stakeholders track KPIs, identify growth opportunities, and analyze sales performance across multiple dimensions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Track revenue and profit trends
- Analyze regional sales performance
- Evaluate product category performance
- Identify top-performing products and customers
- Support data-driven business decisions

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- 💰 Total Sales
- 📈 Total Revenue
- 💵 Total Profit
- 📦 Total Orders
- 🏆 Top Selling Products
- 🌍 Region-wise Sales Analysis
- 📅 Monthly & Yearly Sales Trends
- 🛒 Product Category Performance
- 👥 Customer Analysis
- 🎛️ Interactive Filters & Slicers
